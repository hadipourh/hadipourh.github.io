# GitHub publishing permissions

- Obtain the user's explicit permission before pushing, uploading, or publishing anything to GitHub. Requests to edit, fix, commit, or prepare updates do not grant permission to upload them.
- Local preparation and read-only checks may proceed without upload permission.
- Keep `public/cv/CV.tex` local and excluded from Git. Do not force-add it or remove its ignore rule. Before an authorized push, verify that it is ignored, untracked, and absent from outgoing commits.
